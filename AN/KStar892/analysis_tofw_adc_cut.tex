Due to GEANT limitations to simulate the high-electric fields in materials MC generation is not accurate. 
To circumvent this ADC and efficiency correction was introduced. Efficiency correction value of 0.909 was taken from \href{https://phenix-intra.sdcc.bnl.gov/phenix/WWW/p/draft/belmonrj/AnalysisNotes/ananoterun7.pdf}{AN814}. 
The ADC cut correction was estimated as the ratio of the data lost in TOFw by applying ADC cut ($100 < q_{\text{TOFw}} < 600$) to the full amount of data in TOFw in real data. 
The lower bound of 100 (instead of 60 as in \href{https://phenix-intra.sdcc.bnl.gov/phenix/WWW/p/draft/belmonrj/AnalysisNotes/ananoterun7.pdf}{AN814}) was taken due to worse time-of-flight resolution in $q_{\text{TOFw}} < 100$ region compared to the rest.
The products of TOFw efficiency $\varepsilon_{\text{eff}}^{\text{TOFw}}$ and ADC cut correction $\varepsilon_{\text{ADC}}^{\text{TOFw}}$ were used to re-weight TOFw in MC. Results are shown in Table \ref{table:tofw_adc_corr}.
