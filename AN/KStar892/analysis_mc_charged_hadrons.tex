There is a need for MC predicions of various distributions for fiducial, $E_{\text{loss}}$, and $E_{\text{core}}$ cuts, as well as MC sigmalized residuals calibrations. 
Therefore for each dataset 75 million single particle events were generated using PHENIX exodus. 
For each hadron ($\pi^\pm$, $K^\pm$, $p$, and $\bar{p}$) there were 12.5 million events generated uniformly within $\lvert z_{\text{VTX}}\rvert < 30$, $\lvert\eta\rvert < 0.5$, $0 < \varphi < 2\pi$, from which 10 and 2.5 million events are distributed uniformly within $0.3<p_T<4$ GeV/$c$ and $4.0<p_T<10$ GeV/$c$ respectively.
PHENIX pisa and pisaToDST were used to handle the particle interactions with detectors and track reconstruction respectively.
The uniform $p_T$ distribution of original generated particles was re-weighted to match the measured spectra of charged hadrons (\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.09827}{PPG249}).

The following list describes re-weights introduced for MC of charged hadrons:
\begin{enumerate}
   \item $\alpha$ distribution was re-weighted after the application of fiducial cuts on DC to match the real data.
   \item MC DC and PC1 heatmaps were obtained and $dz_{\text{PC1}}-d\varphi_{\text{PC1}}$ distribution for PC1 was re-weighted to match the real data.
   \item Calibrations of $sdz$ and $sd\varphi$ were performed in MC.
   \item Heatmaps for other detectors were obtained. 
   \item All re-weights mentioned above were recalculated after the application of additional fiducial heatmaps in MC (Sec. \ref{sec:additional_fiducial_cuts}).
\end{enumerate}
