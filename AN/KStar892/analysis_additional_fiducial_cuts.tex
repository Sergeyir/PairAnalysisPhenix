After the application of fiducial cuts there were still inconsistencies found between the heatmaps of real data and MC, especially for PC3w and PC2. To circumvent this problem additional fiducial cuts on bad/dead areas were introduced for MC. These cuts are shown on Fig. \ref{fig:additional_mc_dm_pc1e_run14heau}-\ref{fig:additional_dm_tofe_run15pau}. In order to account for the registering efficiency loss by the introduction of additional fiducial MC cuts, the re-weight was introduced for MC. This re-weight was calculated as a ratio of the amount of data registered in real data after the application of fiducial cuts to the amount of the remaining real data after the application of fiducial and additional MC fiducial cuts.
