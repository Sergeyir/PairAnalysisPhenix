Due to GEANT limitations to simulate the high-electric fields in materials MC generation is not accurate. To circumvent this ADC and efficiency correction was introduced. Efficiency correction value of 0.909 was taken from \href{https://phenix-intra.sdcc.bnl.gov/phenix/WWW/p/draft/belmonrj/AnalysisNotes/ananoterun7.pdf}{AN814}. The ADC cut correction was estimated as the ratio of the data lost in TOFw by applying ADC cut ($60 < q_{\text{TOFw}} < 600$) to the full amount of data in TOFw in real data. The value of ADC cut correction was estimated to be 0.926. The product of efficiency and ADC cut correction (0.842) was used to re-weight TOFw in MC.
